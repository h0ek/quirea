# Quirea

**Follow what matters.**

A lightweight, self-hosted feed reader for following the sources you choose. No ads, AI layer, algorithmic timeline or paid features. Quirea is a hobby project, built for people who want a calm place to read.

**Current status: 0.1.0-dev.** This is a technical bootstrap, not a working feed reader. The web page, basic health endpoints, worker skeleton, migration and installer are available; adding feeds and signing in are not yet implemented.
